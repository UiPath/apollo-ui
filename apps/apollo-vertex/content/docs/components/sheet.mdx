---
title: "Sheet"
description: "Extends the Dialog component to display content that complements the main content."
whenToUse:
  - "the user views or edits a record, a long form, filters or settings in a side panel while the page stays in context."
avoidWhen:
  - when: "the task is short and needs the user's full attention."
    use: dialog
  - when: "the panel is for small screens and should slide up from the bottom and be dismissed with a swipe."
    use: drawer
---
